The Best Car Condos in Wisconsin

Wisconsin has 26 garage condo communities, with most clustered around Green Bay, Milwaukee, Madison and the St. Croix River towns near the Twin Cities. Prices are some of the lowest in the country, and Elkhart Lake gives the state one of America's great trackside options at Road America. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 26 Wisconsin communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Wisconsin?

Woodfield Court Motor Condos in Suamico (101 units) is our top overall pick. For track access, Trackside Road Garage Condos in Elkhart Lake sits near Road America.

How much does a garage condo cost in Wisconsin?

Wisconsin is one of the most affordable markets we track. Our price index puts the median entry price near $148K, and many units sell between $120K and $360K.

Are there garage condos near Road America?

Yes. Trackside Road Garage Condos in Elkhart Lake is near Road America. Units are resale only and rarely listed.
