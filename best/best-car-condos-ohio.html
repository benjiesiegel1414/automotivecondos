The Best Car Condos in Ohio

Ohio has more than 25 garage condo communities, and the market is changing fast. Cincinnati and Columbus are getting purpose-built luxury car suites, and the Lake Erie islands near Port Clinton have one of the largest concentrations of boat and storage condos in the Midwest. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 26 Ohio communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Ohio?

Torque Motor Suites in Blue Ash, north of Cincinnati, is our top pick: 30 deeded suites with 18 ft ceilings and mezzanines that opened in 2026. In Columbus, Horsepower Farms in Powell has a members clubhouse, and The Motor Enclave is planned for New Albany.

How much does a garage condo cost in Ohio?

Our price index puts the Ohio median entry price near $130K, pulled down by Lake Erie storage condos. Luxury car suites in Cincinnati and Columbus run about $300K to $550K.

Are there car condos with a race track in Ohio?

Resolute Motorsports Club is planned in Delaware County with about 250 units. It is pre-construction.
