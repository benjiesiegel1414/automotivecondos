How we picked

We went through all 27 Washington communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club

Seattle, WA · Under construction · 100 units

Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club is the Northwest's first combined collector garage and superyacht marina, on a remediated former fuel terminal on Seattle's Salmon Bay near the Ballard Locks. It is designed by Olson Kundig and is the region's most ambitious garage project.

Why it made the list

The Northwest's first combined collector garage and superyacht marina, on Salmon Bay near the Ballard Locks, with architecture by Olson Kundig. Nothing else in the region compares.

Worth knowing

Priced from the mid $600Ks, with first occupancy targeted for summer 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Washington?

Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club in Seattle is the most ambitious project in the state, pairing collector garages with a superyacht marina. Outside Seattle, Monster Garage Condos in Cle Elum and GarageTown Spokane Valley are strong picks.

Are there car condos in Seattle?

Yes. Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club on Salmon Bay is selling 100 units from the mid $600Ks, with occupancy targeted for 2027. Most other Washington communities are east of the Cascades or north toward Bellingham.

How much does a garage condo cost in Washington?

Our price index puts the Washington median entry price near $248K. Units range from about $100K in resort storage communities to $650K+ in Seattle.
