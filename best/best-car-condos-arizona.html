The Best Car Condos in Arizona

Arizona has more than 70 car condo communities, the second-most in the country, and the market splits in two. Metro Phoenix and Scottsdale are home to some of the best-built luxury garages in the US, with tall ceilings, clubhouses and real collector communities. Lake Havasu City has about 20 storage-style communities built for boats, RVs and side-by-sides. We went through every Arizona community we track and pulled specs, pricing and amenities from developer materials, listings and local reporting. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 71 Arizona communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

1
Best overall builder

Toy Barn North Scottsdale

Scottsdale, AZ · Planned · 64 units

Toy Barn's ninth Valley location is planned for Mack Innovation Park at Loop 101 and Bell Road. It is the company's flagship: 64 suites in phase one, about 135 at build-out, and Toy Barn's first members-only rooftop lounge, with views of the McDowell and Camelback Mountains. Toy Barn has built 543 units across 700,000 sq ft since 2010, and its first seven locations sold out.

Why it made the list

Toy Barn has built 543 units across its Valley locations since 2010 and sold out its first seven. The north Scottsdale flagship adds 20 ft ceilings and the brand's first rooftop lounge. No Arizona builder has a longer track record.

Worth knowing

It is a pre-construction reservation, so delivery is years out. For something sooner, look at Toy Barn resales in Scottsdale, Chandler and Cave Creek.

The Hideaway Luxury Garages

Phoenix, AZ · Planned · 70 units

The Hideaway is a 70-unit gated garage condo community planned for Desert Ridge in north Phoenix, near Loop 101 and Cave Creek Road. Its specs are near the top of the Valley, with 24 to 26 ft interior clearance and 16 x 14 ft doors, plus a three-story owners clubhouse.

Why it made the list

24 to 26 ft interior clearance and 16 x 14 ft doors are near the top of any garage condo in the country. You also get a three-story owners clubhouse in Desert Ridge.

Worth knowing

Reservations only opened in August 2026, and pricing isn't published.

Motor Vault Fountain Hills

Fountain Hills, AZ · Resale only · 30 units

Motor Vault Fountain Hills is a boutique, 30-suite community built by enthusiasts, including the head of the Ford GT Forum. Suites have 19 ft clear height and doors up to 18 ft wide, and a members-only clubhouse with a rooftop patio overlooks the park across the street.

Why it made the list

Just 30 suites with 19 ft ceilings, doors up to 18 ft wide, a rooftop-patio clubhouse and a deionized wash bay. It was built by car people and it shows.

Worth knowing

Resales run about $800K to $900K, and inventory is thin with only 30 owners.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Arizona?

For an established builder, Toy Barn leads Arizona with 543 units built since 2010. For specs, The Hideaway in north Phoenix offers 24 to 26 ft ceilings. For track access, APEX Motor Club near Maricopa has garage condos behind pit lane.

How much does a car condo cost in Arizona?

Arizona has the widest price spread we track. Lake Havasu City storage condos can resell under $100K, metro Phoenix luxury garages typically run $275K to $600K, and boutique Scottsdale communities like Motor Vault have resold around $800K to $900K.

Why are there so many garage condos in Lake Havasu City?

Lake Havasu is a boating and off-road town where HOAs limit what you can park at home, so owners buy deeded storage condos for boats, RVs and side-by-sides. We track about 20 communities there.

Do Arizona car condos have air conditioning?

The luxury communities do. Elite Garages includes a 5-ton HVAC in every suite, and Toy Barn and Motor Vault units are climate controlled. Many storage-style condos are only insulated, so confirm before you buy if you plan to spend time in the unit in summer.

Are there car condos on a race track in Arizona?

Yes. APEX Motor Club near Maricopa has garage condos behind pit lane on its private 2.27-mile road course.
