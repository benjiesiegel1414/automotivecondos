The Best Car Condos in California

California is the most expensive car condo market in the country. Our price index puts the median entry price near $750K, about three times the national median. Inventory is scarce, so the best communities in Orange County, Los Angeles, San Diego and Monterey operate more like private clubs than storage. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 21 California communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

Finish Line Auto Club

Costa Mesa, CA · Established · 40 units

Finish Line Auto Club Costa Mesa, near John Wayne Airport, is Southern California's best-known collector club building. It has about 40 garage condos and a rooftop bar and lounge that look over the runway, and members fly in from Hawaii, Montana and Mexico City.

Best track community

The Thermal Club

Thermal, CA · Established

The Thermal Club is the country's best-known motorsports residential community: about 426 acres in the Coachella Valley with multiple private tracks, where owning a property is how you get track access. Villas come with garages large enough for serious collections.

Why it made the list

About 426 acres of private tracks in the Coachella Valley, with on-site mechanics and coaches. The best-known motorsports residential community in America.

Worth knowing

These are full residences, typically $2.5M to $12M+, not garage condos.

PricingResidences have ranged from about $2.5M to $12M+

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in California?

Finish Line Auto Club in Costa Mesa is our top pick for a collector garage, and The Thermal Club is the top track-based community. Grand Prix Auto Club San Diego is the most exclusive, with only 26 capped residences.

How much does a car condo cost in California?

California is the most expensive market we track, with a median entry price near $750K. Coastal clubs commonly run $750K to $3M+, while Inland Empire projects like The Caves start in the $300s.

Are there car condos with a track in California?

Yes. The Thermal Club in the Coachella Valley has multiple private tracks, and Monterey Motorsport Park has trackside units in Monterey.
