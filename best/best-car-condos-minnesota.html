The Best Car Condos in Minnesota

Minnesota is one of the most developed garage condo markets in the country, with 40+ communities. The reason is simple: long winters and HOAs that limit home garages mean a lot of cars, boats and toys need a heated place to live. The Twin Cities suburbs have everything from the original AutoMotorPlex to 300+ unit storage campuses. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 43 Minnesota communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

AutoMotorPlex Chanhassen

Chanhassen, MN · Resale only · 146 units

AutoMotorPlex is the community that started the car condo trend in Minnesota. Founder Bruno Silikowski, a former Polaris executive, opened in Chanhassen during the Great Recession, and its 146 units have been full for years with hundreds of people on the waitlist. Owners have built out bars, golf simulators, wine cellars and movie rooms.

Why it made the list

The community that started the Minnesota car condo trend. It has 146 units with heated floors and fire suppression, owners who've built bars, sims and wine rooms, and a waitlist reported in the hundreds.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Minnesota?

AutoMotorPlex in Chanhassen is the original and best-known, though it rarely has resales. The Hideout in St. Michael has the best specs (25 ft ceilings, concrete construction), and AutoMotorPlex Medina is the realistic way to buy into the brand.

How much does a garage condo cost in Minnesota?

Our price index puts the Minnesota median entry price near $195K. Most Twin Cities units trade between $185K and $450K, with fully finished luxury suites going higher.

Are Minnesota garage condos heated?

The good ones are. AutoMotorPlex uses heated floors, and Park Place Storage buildings are heated. Always confirm, because heat is the single most important feature in a Minnesota winter.

Why are garage condos so popular in Minnesota?

Long winters, lake culture and HOA rules that limit what you can store at home. Owners keep cars, boats, snowmobiles and RVs in a heated, deeded space close to home.
