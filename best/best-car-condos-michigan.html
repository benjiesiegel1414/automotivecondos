The Best Car Condos in Michigan

Michigan has 35 garage condo communities and two of the most important car country clubs in the US. The market splits between Metro Detroit's motorsport clubs and the Lake Michigan shoreline, where storage condos for boats and summer toys are a way of life. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 35 Michigan communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

M1 Concourse

Pontiac, MI · Established · 250 units

M1 Concourse in Pontiac is one of the largest private garage communities in the world: about 250 garages on 87 acres around the 1.5-mile Champion Motor Speedway. It is also Metro Detroit's busiest car venue, hosting Motor Bella, Roadkill Nights and weekly Cars and Coffee.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Michigan?

M1 Concourse in Pontiac is Michigan's flagship, with about 250 garages around a private 1.5-mile track and major annual events. Motorsports Gateway in Howell is the newer track-based alternative.

How much does a garage condo cost in Michigan?

Our price index puts the Michigan median entry price near $188K. Storage condos along Lake Michigan often trade under $300K, while M1 Concourse and Motorsports Gateway units start around $400K to $600K.

Are there car condos with a track in Michigan?

Yes, two: M1 Concourse (Champion Motor Speedway, 1.5 miles) in Pontiac and Motorsports Gateway in Howell (about 3 miles combined).

Why are there so many storage condos on Lake Michigan?

Summer towns like Grand Haven, Holland and Ludington have strict rules on storing boats and toys at home, so owners buy deeded storage condos close to the water.
