The Best Car Condos in Florida

Florida is the biggest car condo market in the country, with more than 100 communities from Jacksonville to Naples. It is also the market where build quality matters most, because hurricane-rated doors, concrete walls and inland locations are not optional here. We went through every Florida community we track and pulled specs, pricing and amenities from developer materials, listings and local reporting. These are the twelve we'd look at first, each for a different kind of buyer.

How we picked

We went through all 103 Florida communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

1
Best overall

The Motor Enclave

Tampa, FL · Resale only · 300 units

The Motor Enclave is Florida's original car country club: about 200 acres next to Tampa Executive Airport with a 1.5-mile performance track and roughly 300 privately owned garages. It was built by Brad Oleshansky, who also built M1 Concourse in Michigan, and its event business is now one of the biggest in the category.

Why it made the list

It has 200 acres, a 1.5-mile private track, about 300 garages and 42,000 sq ft of event space. The Motor Enclave is the most complete car community in Florida, and a deep resale market makes it easy to get in.

Worth knowing

New inventory is gone, and resales start in the high $300Ks.

UnitsAbout 300 private garages
Unit sizesSmall car suites up to units large enough for RVs and trailers; many include bath and kitchenette
PricingLaunched from about $180K; resales now start in the high $300Ks
DeveloperBrad Oleshansky
Location6500 Motor Enclave Way, Tampa, FL

Amenities

  • 1.5-mile private performance track
  • About 200 acres near I-4 and I-75
  • 42,000 sq ft of event space
  • Large units fit RVs and trailers
  • Sister communities planned in Sarasota and Orlando

2
Best place to live at the track

Circuit Florida Car Condos

Auburndale, FL · Selling · 75 units

Circuit Florida in Auburndale, between Tampa and Orlando, is a true live-at-the-track community. Its 75 townhome-style garage condos are zoned for full-time living and line a 1.7-mile, 14-turn road course with more than 40 feet of elevation change, which is rare in Florida.

Why it made the list

It is the rare Florida garage condo zoned for full-time living, on a 1.7-mile, 14-turn course with real elevation change. Members get about 160 track days a year, two clubhouses and on-site mechanics.

Worth knowing

Expect about an $80K initiation and roughly $800 a month in dues on top of the unit price.

Unit sizes838 sq ft (Monza loft) up to 2,688 sq ft (Le Mans, 2 bed / 2 bath)
PricingAbout $475K to $1.8M, fully furnished; club initiation about $80K plus about $800/month dues
LocationChicane Cir, Auburndale, FL

Amenities

  • 1.7-mile, 14-turn private road course
  • About 160 member track days a year
  • Zoned for full-time residence
  • Two clubhouses including a spa and restaurant
  • Sim rigs, coaching and on-site mechanics
  • Lifts can double capacity up to 12 cars per unit

3
Biggest motorsports country club

P1 Motor Club

Port St. Lucie, FL · Under construction

P1 Motor Club is the biggest motorsports country club being built in Florida: 650 acres in St. Lucie County with a 3-mile track, a planned 4.5-mile circuit and almost 10 miles of driving surface at full build-out. Garage townhomes put car space downstairs and living space upstairs, overlooking the track.

Why it made the list

650 acres, a 3-mile track with a 4.5-mile circuit coming, karting, drift and off-road areas, plus garage townhomes over the track. Nothing else in the Southeast is being built at this scale.

Worth knowing

Membership starts around $192K, residences are about $1.4M, and the real estate is still years from completion.

UnitsAbout 200 residences planned (68 sold so far)
PricingReal estate about $1.4M (members only); membership from about $192K plus annual dues
LocationOkeechobee Rd, Port St. Lucie area, FL
TimelineFirst track opening late 2026; real estate construction around early 2027

Amenities

  • 3-mile track, with a 4.5-mile circuit planned
  • Karting, rally, drift and off-road areas
  • Clubhouse with rooftop bar, pool and courts
  • Mercedes-AMG partner fleet for members
  • About 280 members

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Florida?

We rank The Motor Enclave in Tampa first overall for its private track, scale and event space. If you want to live at the track, Circuit Florida in Auburndale is the pick. For ultra-luxury collector storage, CollectionSuites in Doral leads.

How much does a car condo cost in Florida?

Florida car condos we track run from under $200K for storage-style units in Fort Myers and Punta Gorda to $1M+ in Miami and Naples. Most luxury units trade between $350K and $700K.

Are Florida car condos hurricane proof?

No building is hurricane proof, but the better Florida communities use concrete or tilt-wall construction, hurricane-rated doors and inland sites. Indian River MotorHaus 3.0, for example, is built with 7-inch tilt-wall concrete to hurricane-shelter standards. Always ask for the wind rating of the doors.

Can you live in a car condo in Florida?

Most Florida garage condos don't allow overnight stays. Exceptions include Circuit Florida, which is zoned for full-time residence, Sebring Motor Garages, which allows overnight stays, and garage townhomes at P1 Motor Club.

Where are most car condos in Florida?

Southwest Florida leads, with Naples, Fort Myers, Sarasota and Punta Gorda. Tampa Bay, the Treasure Coast and Palm Beach County follow.
