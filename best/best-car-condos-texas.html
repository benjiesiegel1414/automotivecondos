The Best Car Condos in Texas

We went through every Texas community we track and pulled specs, pricing and amenities from developer materials, listings and local reporting. These are the ten we'd look at first, each for a different kind of buyer.

How we picked

We went through all 52 Texas communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

1
Best overall

Maranello Luxury Auto Garages

Plano, TX · Selling · 79 units

Maranello is the most ambitious garage condo project in North Texas: a 200,000 sq ft, inward-facing building on 11+ acres off the George Bush Turnpike in west Plano. Each of the 79 deeded garages opens through a glass door onto a 60,000 sq ft open-air concourse, so the whole building works like a private car show that never closes.

Why it made the list

Nothing else in Texas combines a 60,000 sq ft show concourse, a 13,000 sq ft lounge and on-site detailing, PPF and paint correction under one roof. If you want your garage to also be your social club, this is it.

Worth knowing

You pay for it: about $650 per sq ft, roughly double many DFW alternatives.

Unit sizes1,000 to 2,000 sq ft (most 25 ft wide, 40 to 80 ft deep)
DoorsGlass garage doors facing the interior concourse

  • App-controlled HVAC and full utilities per unit
  • On-site service center, detailing and paint correction
  • Window tint, PPF and bodywork on site
  • Professional photo studio

Best track access

T11 Car Condos at COTA

Austin, TX · Selling

T11 sits at Turn 11 of Circuit of the Americas, the Formula 1 track southeast of Austin, and is built by the circuit itself. Owners get a private race viewing platform and dedicated track time, which no other Texas garage condo can match.

Why it made the list

It is the only Texas garage condo built by an F1 circuit, with a private viewing platform at Turn 11 and dedicated owner track time. Units also scale from 1,500 to 6,000 sq ft.

Worth knowing

It is 20+ minutes from central Austin, and large finished suites run well into seven figures.

Most proven builder

Garages of America — Plano

Plano, TX · Established

Garages of America (formerly Garages of Texas) is the most proven garage condo builder in the state. It has opened 13 communities across Texas and sold about 850 garages since its Plano flagship broke ground in 2014. That first site sold all 69 units before construction finished.

Why it made the list

13 Texas communities and about 850 garages sold. If you want a deep resale market and an HOA that already runs events, Garages of America has the longest track record in the state.

Worth knowing

Most locations are resale only now, so inventory depends on what owners list.

Units69 at the Plano flagship; about 850 sold across 13 Texas communities
Unit sizes800 to 1,750 sq ft, fully customizable
PricingResales across the brand commonly list from the mid $300Ks to $1.3M+
DeveloperGarages of America (founders Fred Gans and Jack Griffin)

Amenities

  • 13 locations across DFW, Houston and Austin
  • Owner HOAs that host events
  • Monthly first-Saturday car showcase at the Carrollton site
  • Units built out as lounges, gyms and wet bars by owners

Garages of the Americas

Del Valle, TX · Selling · 200 units

Garages of the Americas is an 18-acre campus directly across from Circuit of the Americas, developed by racer Tim Myers. It is the volume play near COTA: around 200 climate-controlled units, with about half already sold and several buyers taking multiple units.

Why it made the list

It is right across from Circuit of the Americas with about 200 units, tall mezzanines that clear trailers and lifts, plus detailers and a racing coach on site, usually at a lower price than living inside the circuit.

Worth knowing

It is a large, busy campus, so it feels less exclusive than a 40-unit boutique.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Texas?

For an all-around luxury experience we rank Maranello in Plano first, for its 60,000 sq ft concourse, lounge and on-site services. For track access, T11 at Circuit of the Americas is the clear pick. For value, look at Shavano in San Antonio or Big Tex in Tomball.

How much does a car condo cost in Texas?

Texas car condos we track run from about $185K for storage-style units near Houston to $2M+ for large finished suites at COTA. Most luxury units in DFW, Houston and Austin trade between $350K and $900K.

Where are most car condos in Texas?

Dallas-Fort Worth has the most, led by the Garages of America communities in Plano, Frisco, Allen, Carrollton and beyond. Houston and Austin are the next biggest markets, followed by San Antonio.

Can you live in a Texas car condo?

Generally no. Most Texas garage condos are zoned commercial or industrial, so overnight stays aren't allowed, even though many owners add bathrooms, bars and lounges.

Are there Texas car condos with a race track?

Yes. T11 and Garages of the Americas are at or across from Circuit of the Americas in Austin, and G2 Motorsports Park in Anna has garages on a private road course.
