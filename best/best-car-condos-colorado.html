The Best Car Condos in Colorado

Colorado has nearly 60 garage condo communities, most of them along the Front Range between Fort Collins and Colorado Springs. Prices are lower than you'd expect for a market this strong, with many metro Denver units under $350K. The best communities stand out on build quality: tall ceilings for lifts, real heat and clubhouses. We went through every Colorado community we track and pulled specs and pricing from developer materials and listings. These are the eight we'd look at first.

How we picked

We went through all 57 Colorado communities in our directory. We weighed build quality and specs (ceiling height, door size, construction), amenities and clubhouse, track access, location, price per square foot, developer track record and whether you can actually buy in today. No community paid to be on this list. Specs and prices come from developer materials, listings and local reporting, and the sources are linked under each pick. Prices move fast, so confirm the current numbers before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car condo in Colorado?

For specs, Citadel Luxury Garages in Glenwood Springs includes 23 ft ceilings, 14 x 18 ft doors, HVAC and a private bath standard. In metro Denver, Titan Car Condos (27 ft ceilings) and Garage Point Colorado (clubhouse, 89 units) are our top picks.

How much does a garage condo cost in Colorado?

Most Colorado garage condos we track start between $175K and $450K. Our price index puts the Colorado median entry price near $240K, well below the national median.

Where are most garage condos in Colorado?

Along the Front Range: the south Denver suburbs (Littleton, Centennial, Castle Rock, Parker), the north corridor (Longmont, Loveland, Windsor, Johnstown) and Colorado Springs.

Are Colorado garage condos heated?

Many are, but not all. The Garage Society uses natural gas heat, Citadel includes installed HVAC, and Titan units have gas in the unit. Always confirm heat before buying in Colorado.

Can you live in a Colorado garage condo?

Generally no. They are zoned commercial or industrial, so overnight stays aren't allowed even when a unit has a bathroom.
