Idaho's garage condo market has grown up alongside the Treasure Valley boom. As Boise, Meridian and Eagle have added new residents, many of them moving from larger Western metros with a truck, a boat, a trailer, a side-by-side and a car or two, the demand for secure, deeded storage has outpaced what newer subdivisions and their HOAs allow at home. The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 11 garage condo and car condo communities in Idaho, eight of them in the Treasure Valley.

Idaho is a market of sold-out first phases and new projects coming behind them. Five of the 11 communities trade on resale only, several early Boise projects are listed as sold out, and the newest projects in Meridian and North Idaho are priced in the $250K to $315K range for the units that publish pricing. This guide walks through every community, what it costs, how to buy into a sold-out market, and what Idaho-specific factors should shape your decision. All figures come from the full Idaho directory, also available on the interactive map.

Where Idaho's Garage Condos Are

Boise has the most communities in the state. Three of them carry the Falcon Storage Condos name: Falcon Storage Condos, Falcon Storage Condos Boise and Falcon Storage Condos East Boise. All three trade on resale only, and the Boise and East Boise locations are listed as sold out. That is a strong signal of demand: buyers who want into these projects need to watch for resales or get on an interest list.

Go To My Garage is an established Boise community with 20 units. LuxeLocker Boise is selling and offers both purchase and rental, which makes it one of the easiest ways to get into a Boise unit if you want to try the lifestyle before committing to buy. Meridian (2 communities)

Meridian, Boise's fast-growing western neighbor, has the two largest projects that publish unit counts. Ten Mile Storage Condos is under construction with 52 units and pre-sale pricing from $270,000. Club Storage Condos is selling 49 units on an inquire basis. Between them, these two projects account for 101 of the 121 known units in the state. Browse both on the page for garage condos in Meridian.

Eagle (1 community)

Eagle Storage Condos trades on resale only with resales from $218K to $315K, the only Idaho community in the directory with a published resale range. Eagle is one of the Treasure Valley's most affluent suburbs and sits close to the Boise River and the foothills, so buyers here often store boats, RVs, side-by-sides and collector cars together.

North Idaho: Coeur d'Alene and Post Falls (2 communities)

North Idaho is a lake and mountain market, built around Lake Coeur d'Alene and close to Spokane across the Washington line. GarageTown USA Coeur d'Alene trades on resales. In Post Falls, Locked and Loaded Garage Condos is selling 20' x 50' units from about $250,000. A 50-foot-deep unit is a meaningful size: it can typically fit a boat on a trailer or a long truck, or two cars nose to tail, though you should always confirm with your own measurements.

Magic Valley (1 community)

LuxeLocker Magic Valley in Jerome, just north of Twin Falls across the Snake River Canyon, is selling with both purchase and rental options. It is the only community in the directory serving south central Idaho.

Who Buys Garage Condos in Idaho

Idaho's buyers reflect the state's outdoor lifestyle as much as its car culture:

  • Boat and wake owners. Lake Coeur d'Alene, Lake Pend Oreille, Lucky Peak, Lake Cascade and the Snake River give Idaho a huge boating population, and many newer subdivisions do not allow boats or trailers to be stored outside.
  • RV, camper and toy hauler owners. Long, tall vehicles are one of the hardest things to store in the Treasure Valley, and they drive demand for deep units with tall doors.
  • Powersports owners. Side-by-sides, dirt bikes, snowmobiles and ATVs are everywhere in Idaho. A garage condo keeps the machines, trailers, tools and gear in one place.
  • Car collectors and enthusiasts. The Boise area has an active car scene, and buyers relocating from California, Washington and other Western states often arrive with collector cars and few places to keep them.
  • Small business owners. Contractors, tradespeople and online sellers use units for equipment and inventory where declarations allow it.

Idaho-Specific Buying Considerations

Door height, depth and RV fit

Because so many Idaho buyers store boats on trailers, toy haulers and RVs, unit dimensions matter more here than in a pure car condo market. Ask for exact door width and height, clear ceiling height and depth. Measure your rig with the tower, antennas, AC units and bikes in place, not just the brochure height. Locked and Loaded publishes its 20' x 50' unit format; for other projects, ask for a floor plan.

Winter and summer extremes

The Treasure Valley has hot, dry summers and cold winters, and North Idaho gets real snow. Ask whether units are insulated, whether heaters or mini-splits are permitted, who handles snow removal and how quickly drive aisles are cleared. In summer, an uninsulated metal building can get very hot, which matters for batteries, tires, interiors and any fluids you store.

Buying into a sold-out market

With several Boise projects sold out and five communities resale only, you need a plan:

  • Register interest with each resale-only community and ask whether the association or developer keeps a buyer list.
  • Bookmark the full Idaho directory and check community pages regularly for new resale availability.
  • Get pre-approved or line up cash so you can move quickly; desirable resales may not stay available long.
  • Consider reserving at Ten Mile Storage Condos while it is under construction, or renting at LuxeLocker while you wait for the right unit.

Pre-construction purchases

If you reserve at Ten Mile Storage Condos or another project before completion, read the purchase agreement closely. Confirm how your deposit is held, what the completion timeline is, what happens if the project is delayed, and exactly which specifications (door size, power, insulation, plumbing, mezzanine) are included. The buying guide covers the full checklist.

Rental and use rules

Most declarations prohibit living in a unit and restrict hazardous materials. Some allow business use and rentals; others do not. If you plan to rent your unit part of the year, confirm the rules first and read our renting guide. If you already own a unit, you can list your unit on AutomotiveCondos.com for sale or rent.

Renting vs. Buying in Idaho

Idaho is one of the few states in the directory where renting from an operator is a realistic path, thanks to the two LuxeLocker locations in Boise and Jerome. That gives buyers a genuine choice, and the right answer depends on a few questions:

  • How long will you stay? If you expect to keep the unit for many years, ownership lets you build equity and customize the space with lifts, cabinets, flooring and lighting. If you are new to the area or unsure where you will settle in the Treasure Valley, renting first keeps you flexible.
  • How much will you customize? Renters typically cannot make permanent improvements. Owners can, within the declaration's rules, and a well-finished unit is often easier to resell.
  • What is the full cost comparison? Compare a year of rent against a year of ownership costs: financing or opportunity cost on the purchase price, HOA dues, property taxes, insurance and power. Our cost guide walks through the math.
  • Is the project you want sold out? If the community you prefer has no resale available, renting nearby can bridge the gap until a unit comes up.

Many owners in Idaho also rent out their units for part of the year, particularly in winter when boats and summer toys are put away and other owners need extra space. If that is part of your plan, confirm the declaration allows it before you buy.

How to Choose an Idaho Garage Condo

  • Start with distance. Pick projects within a short drive of home or the lake. Boise, Meridian and Eagle buyers have eight options within the Treasure Valley.
  • Match the unit to your biggest vehicle. Your longest trailer or tallest RV decides which projects even qualify.
  • Decide new or resale. New units at Club Storage, Locked and Loaded, LuxeLocker or Ten Mile; resales at Falcon, Eagle Storage and GarageTown USA.
  • Read the documents. Declaration, bylaws, rules, budget, reserves and minutes.
  • Compare total cost. Price, dues, taxes, power and insurance over the time you expect to own it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many garage condo communities are there in Idaho?

The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 11 garage condo and car condo communities in Idaho as of November 2026: five in Boise, two in Meridian, one in Eagle, one in Coeur d'Alene, one in Post Falls and one in Jerome. Four are selling, five are resale only, one is established and one is under construction.

How much does a garage condo cost in Boise?

Published Treasure Valley pricing in the directory includes resales from $218K to $315K at Eagle Storage Condos and pre-sale pricing from $270,000 at Ten Mile Storage Condos in Meridian. Most Boise communities price on an inquire basis, and LuxeLocker Boise offers both purchase and rental.

Are Boise garage condos sold out?

Several are. Falcon Storage Condos Boise and Falcon Storage Condos East Boise are listed as sold out and trade on resale only, as does the original Falcon Storage Condos. Buyers can still purchase new units at LuxeLocker Boise, Club Storage Condos in Meridian and Ten Mile Storage Condos (under construction).

Can I rent a garage condo in Idaho instead of buying?

Yes. LuxeLocker Boise and LuxeLocker Magic Valley in Jerome both offer purchase and rental options. Some individual owners in other communities also rent their units, subject to each association's rules.

Are there garage condos in Coeur d'Alene and North Idaho?

Yes. GarageTown USA Coeur d'Alene trades on resales, and Locked and Loaded Garage Condos in Post Falls is selling 20' x 50' units from about $250,000. Both serve boat, RV and car owners around Lake Coeur d'Alene and the Spokane area.
