Buying a Car Condo: 9 Things to Check Before You Sign

November 6, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

Car condos look simple — four walls and a big door — but the details decide whether the unit actually works for you. Run this checklist before you sign anything.

1. Measure the door, not the building

A 12-foot door handles cars and trailers. A 14-foot door clears most enclosed trailers and Class C motorhomes. A Class A motorhome wants 16 feet. The brochure says "RV storage"; the door says the truth.

2. Confirm clear ceiling height

Two-post lifts want about 12 feet of clear height; four-post storage lifts want 14+. Measure under the lowest obstruction — sprinkler lines, lights, door tracks — not to the roof peak.

3. Read the declaration before you dream

Some communities allow running a business, some allow a loft lounge, very few allow overnight stays — and the rules live in the condo declaration and city zoning, not the sales pitch. If your plan is "shop by day, man-cave by night," confirm both allow it.

4. Check the power

A lift, a welder, two A/C units and an EV charger add up. Ask what service the unit has (100A? 200A?) and what upgrading costs.

5. Ask what the dues cover — and read the reserve study

Dues are usually reasonable, but ask what happens when the roof needs replacing. A community with no reserves is a special assessment waiting to happen.

6. Insurance: structure vs. contents

The association insures the shell. Your cars, your lift, your mezzanine build-out need your own policy. Get a quote before closing, not after.

7. Understand the phase you're buying into

Early-phase pre-construction pricing is usually the community's lowest ever — but delivery dates slip. If you need the space this year, a finished resale beats a rendering.

8. Visit on a Saturday

The community is half the product. Show up when owners are around: if the culture is cars-and-coffee and open doors, you'll know in ten minutes. If it's dead silent rows of closed doors, you'll know that too.

9. Think resale from day one

Generic, well-located units with tall doors resell fastest. Ultra-personalized build-outs can narrow your buyer pool. You don't have to build for the next owner — just know what you're choosing.
