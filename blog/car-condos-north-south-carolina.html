Car Condos in North and South Carolina: 2026 Market Guide

November 7, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

The Carolinas are one of the fastest-developing car condo regions in the Southeast. The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 19 car and garage condo communities across the two states: 11 in North Carolina and 8 in South Carolina. Unlike mature markets such as Florida or Arizona, much of the Carolinas inventory is new: 6 of North Carolina's 11 communities are still in the planned stage, and 3 of South Carolina's 8 are under construction. That means buyers here are mostly choosing between new construction and pre-construction reservations, with very little resale inventory to compare against.

The market clusters in two places. The Charlotte metro, including Concord, Mooresville, Cornelius, and Fort Mill just across the South Carolina line, holds 9 of the 19 communities and leans heavily on the region's NASCAR heritage. The Charleston area holds 4 more, led by The Vault Luxury Motor Condos. If you are searching "car condos Charlotte NC," "garage condos Lake Norman," or "car condos Charleston SC," this guide covers every community in both states. See the full listings in the North Carolina directory and the South Carolina directory, or browse them on the interactive map.

Why the Carolinas Are a Car Condo Market

Racing culture runs deep. The Charlotte region is the center of American stock car racing. Charlotte Motor Speedway sits in Concord, Mooresville has long called itself "Race City USA" because of the race team shops and motorsports businesses based there, and the NASCAR Hall of Fame is in uptown Charlotte. That concentration of drivers, crew members, engineers, fabricators, and fans creates a local buyer pool that understands why a car deserves its own garage. It is no accident that the directory's Charlotte-area communities include a motor club (Ten Tenths Motor Club in Concord) and a planned social club tied to a "Race City" name in Mooresville.

Lake Norman and the toy economy. Lake Norman, the largest man-made lake in North Carolina, is ringed by Mooresville, Cornelius, Davidson, and Huntersville. Lakefront and lake-area homeowners own boats, wake boats, jet skis, and golf carts, and HOA rules in many lake communities limit what can be parked outside. Garage condos near the lake solve that problem, which is why three of the directory's North Carolina communities are in Mooresville and one is in Cornelius.

Population growth. Both states have been among the faster-growing in the country, with steady in-migration to Charlotte, Raleigh, Greenville, and the Charleston area. New arrivals often come from markets where car condos are already familiar, and newer suburban housing tends to have HOA restrictions and modest garages. That combination creates demand for secure off-site storage.

Coastal lifestyle and weather. Along the South Carolina coast, summer humidity, salt air, and hurricane season make enclosed, elevated, well-built storage especially valuable for collectible cars. Buyers in Charleston, Mount Pleasant, and Bluffton are often protecting cars that cannot be garaged at a downtown or island home.

For a buyer, the takeaway is simple: the Carolinas are a new-construction market, and pricing reflects new-construction quality and amenities.

North Carolina: Charlotte, Concord, and Lake Norman

Concord

Concord is home to Charlotte Motor Speedway and the most active car condo cluster in North Carolina, with three communities. If you are researching car condos in Concord:

  • Storage Caves Concord: selling; pricing on inquiry. Storage Caves is a luxury garage condo developer with multiple projects in the Southeast.
  • Storage Caves Concord II: planned, with reservations open. A second phase is a good signal that the first found buyers.
  • Ten Tenths Motor Club: an established motor club community. Inquire for availability.

Mooresville and Cornelius (Lake Norman)

Mooresville sits at the north end of Lake Norman and is closely associated with the race team industry. For car condos in Mooresville, there are three communities:

  • The Garages at LKN: selling from $360,000, the only Charlotte-area community in the directory with a published starting price.
  • Storage Caves Lake Norman: planned, listed as coming soon.
  • Race City Motorpark Social Club: planned, with a social club concept; inquire for details.

On the east side of the lake, Chartown Drive Showroom Condos in Cornelius is resale only. Showroom-style condos typically emphasize display space and finished interiors, which appeals to collectors who want to show cars as much as store them.

Charlotte

Inside the city of Charlotte, Auto ClubHouse Charlotte is planned and in pre-construction. The Auto ClubHouse brand also operates in Palm Beach County, Florida, where its Jupiter location is selling from $615,000 according to the directory, which gives a sense of the tier this concept targets. Charlotte pricing has not been published yet, so treat that as context rather than a quote.

North Carolina Beyond Charlotte

Raleigh and the Sandhills

The Triangle has one community in the directory: Carolina Exotic Car Club in Raleigh, which is planned. Given the size and growth of the Raleigh, Durham, and Cary market, it is notable how little dedicated car condo supply exists there, which makes it a market to watch.

Southwest of the Triangle in Moore County, Autoport Luxury Driving Resort in Robbins is planned and built around garage villas at a driving resort. For buyers who want track access attached to their garage, resort-style communities like this are a different product from a storage condo, combining a garage with driving facilities and a club membership.

The Brunswick County Coast

Village Point Landing in Bolivia is selling, with pricing on inquiry. Brunswick County sits between Wilmington and Myrtle Beach, with beach towns that attract retirees and second-home owners who often need extra storage for cars, boats, and golf carts.

South Carolina: Charleston and the Lowcountry

The Charleston area is South Carolina's most developed car condo market, and it is anchored by The Vault Luxury Motor Condos, which has two of its three South Carolina locations here.

  • The Vault Luxury Motor Condos (Daniel Island) in Charleston: selling from $450,000, the highest published starting price among the directory's Charleston-area communities.
  • The Vault Luxury Motor Condos (Mount Pleasant): selling from $395,000.
  • Motor District on Johns Island: under construction, from $395,000.
  • Awendaw Garage Condos: selling, pricing on inquiry. Awendaw sits north of Mount Pleasant along US-17 and is a quieter, more rural option.

The $55,000 gap between The Vault's Daniel Island and Mount Pleasant locations reflects location as much as product. Daniel Island is a planned island community within the city of Charleston with limited commercial land, and premium locations close to high-end residential neighborhoods carry premium prices. In the Lowcountry, also ask about flood zone designation and elevation before you buy; it affects insurance and peace of mind during hurricane season.

Bluffton and Hilton Head

CarVillage USA in Bluffton has 31 units under construction, priced from $550,000, the highest published starting price in either Carolina. Bluffton sits next to Hilton Head Island, a resort and retirement market with many high-end collectors and limited garage space in gated island communities.

South Carolina: Upstate and the Charlotte Suburbs

Fort Mill

Storage Caves Fort Mill is under construction with 119 units, by far the largest known unit count in the Carolinas directory. Fort Mill is part of the Charlotte metro, just across the state line in York County, so it effectively serves Charlotte buyers. Together with the three North Carolina Storage Caves projects, this brings the developer's Carolinas footprint to four communities. Pricing is available on inquiry.

Greenville and Lake Keowee

The Upstate has two communities. The Vault Luxury Motor Condos (Greenville) is selling from $335,000, which makes it the lowest-priced Vault location and a good value relative to the Charleston sites. Greenville has a strong automotive identity thanks to its manufacturing base and has been one of the faster-growing metros in the state.

Motor-Vault Lakeside at Lake Keowee in Six Mile is selling from $285,000, the lowest published entry price in the Carolinas directory. Lake Keowee is a mountain lake with upscale lakefront communities, and garage space here serves boat owners as well as car collectors.

Buying Pre-Construction in the Carolinas

Because so much of this market is planned or under construction, the details of a pre-construction purchase matter more here than in most states. Before you put down a deposit, confirm:

  • Deposit terms: how much is due at reservation and at contract, whether it is held in escrow, and under what conditions it is refundable.
  • Completion timeline: the expected delivery date, the outside date in the contract, and your options if construction runs late.
  • Specifications: unit dimensions, door width and height, ceiling height (important if you want a lift or mezzanine), electrical service, insulation, and climate control.
  • Association documents: the draft declaration, bylaws, rules, and the projected HOA budget, including what dues cover and how reserves will be funded.
  • Use restrictions: rules on overnight stays, business use, leasing, and buildout modifications.
  • Club structure: for motor club and resort concepts like Ten Tenths, Race City Motorpark Social Club, and Autoport, whether membership is mandatory, what it costs, and how it transfers on resale.

Renting Out a Carolinas Unit

With demand from collectors, boat owners near Lake Norman and Lake Keowee, and coastal residents with limited garage space, rental demand should be healthy, but supply of comparable units is still forming. If you are buying partly as an investment, confirm the association allows leasing and research what climate-controlled vehicle storage costs nearby. Our renting guide covers how to set rent, write a lease, and handle insurance.

How to Choose a Carolinas Market

  • Racing culture and the most choice: Concord and Mooresville in the Charlotte metro.
  • Boat and lake lifestyle: The Garages at LKN and Storage Caves Lake Norman near Lake Norman, and Motor-Vault Lakeside at Lake Keowee.
  • Lowest published entry price: Motor-Vault Lakeside at Lake Keowee from $285,000, followed by The Vault Greenville from $335,000.
  • Coastal luxury: The Vault on Daniel Island and in Mount Pleasant, Motor District on Johns Island, and CarVillage USA in Bluffton.
  • Largest new project: Storage Caves Fort Mill, with 119 units under construction.
  • Track and resort access: Autoport Luxury Driving Resort in Robbins and Ten Tenths Motor Club in Concord.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many car condo communities are there in North and South Carolina?

The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 19 communities across the two states as of November 2026: 11 in North Carolina and 8 in South Carolina. Nine are in the Charlotte metro (including Fort Mill, SC), four are in the Charleston area, and the rest are in Upstate South Carolina, Raleigh, the Sandhills, and along the southern coast.

Are there car condos in Charlotte, NC?

Yes. The Charlotte metro has nine communities in the directory. Concord has Storage Caves Concord (selling), Storage Caves Concord II (reservations open), and Ten Tenths Motor Club (established). Mooresville has The Garages at LKN (from $360,000), Storage Caves Lake Norman, and Race City Motorpark Social Club. Cornelius has Chartown Drive Showroom Condos, Charlotte has the planned Auto ClubHouse Charlotte, and Fort Mill, SC has Storage Caves Fort Mill with 119 units under construction.

How much does a car condo cost in Charleston, SC?

Published starting prices in the Charleston area run from $395,000 to $450,000. The Vault Luxury Motor Condos starts from $395,000 in Mount Pleasant and from $450,000 on Daniel Island, and Motor District on Johns Island starts from $395,000. Awendaw Garage Condos is also selling, with pricing on inquiry.

What is the cheapest car condo in the Carolinas?

Among communities that publish pricing, Motor-Vault Lakeside at Lake Keowee in Six Mile, SC has the lowest starting price at $285,000, followed by The Vault Luxury Motor Condos in Greenville from $335,000. Several other communities, including Storage Caves Concord, Village Point Landing, and Awendaw Garage Condos, list pricing on inquiry, so contact them for current numbers.

Is it safe to buy a pre-construction car condo in the Carolinas?

Pre-construction can be a good way to get new product and choose your unit, and most of the Carolinas market is new construction. Protect yourself by confirming that deposits are held in escrow, reading the completion and outside dates in the contract, reviewing the draft association documents and budget, and verifying unit specifications like door height and ceiling clearance before you sign.
