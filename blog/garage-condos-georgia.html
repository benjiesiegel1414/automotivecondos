Garage Condos in Georgia: 2026 Market Guide

November 7, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

Metro Atlanta has one of the most active car cultures in the Southeast, and its garage condo market is finally catching up. The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 12 garage condo and car condo communities in Georgia. Eight of them sit in Atlanta's northern suburbs, along the GA 400, I-75 and I-85 corridors, and the rest are destination projects tied to a racetrack, a mountain town, a lake or the Chattanooga metro.

Georgia's market is young and selling. Nine of the 12 communities are actively selling from the developer, which is a much higher share than in older storage condo markets in the Midwest, where many projects are resale only. Prices are also higher than in those markets, with most published entry points between $150K and $375K and the top of published ranges reaching $850K. This guide covers where Georgia's 12 communities are, what they cost, and how to choose. All figures come from the full Georgia directory, which you can also browse on the interactive map.

Georgia Garage Condos at a Glance

  • 12 communities in 9 cities, from Ringgold near the Tennessee line to Greensboro on Lake Oconee.
  • 9 are selling from the developer, 1 is resale only, 1 is under construction and 1 is established.
  • 72 known units across the 2 communities that publish a count (Storage Caves Buford with 44 and Ball Ground Garage Condos with 28). Most Georgia projects do not publish a unit count, so the true total is meaningfully higher.
  • Published starting prices run from $98K to $375K, and the top of published ranges reaches $850K at Alpharetta Personal Warehouse.
  • Acworth, Alpharetta and Buford each have 2 communities, making them the most active garage condo cities in the state.

If you are searching for garage condos in Atlanta, the short version is that the action is in the northern suburbs, the inventory is mostly new construction, and you should expect to talk directly with developers because pricing and availability change quickly.

Alpharetta and the GA 400 Corridor

Alpharetta is the center of North Fulton's car culture, with a large population of enthusiasts, collectors and business owners and a long tradition of weekend car meets. The directory lists two Alpharetta communities. Alpharetta Personal Warehouse is selling units from $375K to $850K, the highest published prices in Georgia. Those figures reflect the location and the larger "personal warehouse" format, which often suits buyers who want room for several vehicles, a lounge or a hobby workshop. Stables Motor Condos in Alpharetta trades on resale only, and it is one of the few Georgia communities in the directory where both buying and renting are options. Browse every Alpharetta listing on the page for garage condos in Alpharetta.

Farther up the corridor in Cherokee County, Ball Ground Garage Condos is selling 28 units from $325K to $400K. Ball Ground sits within reach of the North Georgia foothills and mountain roads, which makes it attractive to drivers who want to keep a weekend car close to the roads they actually enjoy.

Acworth and the I-75 Corridor

Acworth, in northwest metro Atlanta near Lake Allatoona, has two communities. Garage Lofts at Acworth Summit is selling units from $225K to $500K, and Seven Hills Car Condos is selling on an inquire basis. The Lake Allatoona connection means many buyers here store boats and personal watercraft alongside cars. See all garage condos in Acworth.

Buford, Braselton and the I-85 Corridor

Gwinnett County and the I-85 corridor northeast of Atlanta form the third cluster. Buford, next to Lake Lanier, has two communities: Storage Caves Buford is selling 44 units from $98K to $725K, the widest price range in the state and the lowest published starting price, and Garage Lofts at Mall of Georgia is under construction. Browse all garage condos in Buford.

A short drive up I-85, Stables Motor Condos (Braselton) is selling. Braselton is home to Michelin Raceway Road Atlanta, one of the best-known road courses in the country and the home of the Petit Le Mans endurance race, so a garage here appeals to track-day drivers and racing fans as much as to local collectors.

The North Georgia Mountains

Two destination projects serve drivers who want to be in the mountains. Atlanta Motorsports Park Trackside Garage Condos in Dawsonville is an established community with units from $225K, set at a private motorsports country club. Owning a garage at a track means you can store a dedicated track car, trailer and tools on site and drive straight onto the circuit. Farther north, The Toy Barn of Dillard is selling from $295K in Rabun County near the North Carolina line, a natural base for mountain driving, motorcycles, boats and off-road vehicles.

Lake Oconee and the Chattanooga Area

East of Atlanta, Park Court Garage Condos in Greensboro serves the Lake Oconee community, a lake and golf destination with many second homes. Expect demand for boat, golf cart and second-car storage. In the far northwest corner of the state, HomeBase Flex Garage Condos in Ringgold is selling from $150K, giving buyers in the Chattanooga metro a Georgia-side option.

Four communities also publish the top of their range: Alpharetta Personal Warehouse ($850K), Storage Caves Buford ($725K), Garage Lofts at Acworth Summit ($500K) and Ball Ground Garage Condos ($400K). Those spreads tell you that unit sizes vary enormously within a single project. A starting price usually buys a smaller single-car or two-car bay; the top of the range typically buys a large unit with extra depth, height and a finished mezzanine.

What this means for buyers:

  • Entry-level options exist, but they are limited. Storage Caves Buford ($98K start) and HomeBase Flex in Ringgold ($150K start) are the most accessible published entry points.
  • The core of the market is $225K to $375K. Five of the seven published starting prices fall in this band.
  • Alpharetta commands a premium. Its published range starts higher and ends higher than any other Georgia community.
  • Five communities price on request. Seven Hills, Stables Motor Condos (both locations), Garage Lofts at Mall of Georgia and Park Court do not publish pricing, so ask for current availability and recent sales.

Why Atlanta Buyers Are Moving to Garage Condos

Several forces are pushing metro Atlanta enthusiasts toward deeded garage space:

  • Suburban HOAs. Many North Fulton, Forsyth, Cherokee and Gwinnett neighborhoods restrict what you can park in a driveway or do in your garage, and they often prohibit boats, trailers and project cars stored outside. A garage condo moves those vehicles off your property and out of your HOA's line of sight.
  • Home garages are full. Two-car and three-car home garages fill up fast with daily drivers, bikes and household storage. A dedicated unit gives collectors space to display, detail and maintain cars properly.
  • Lakes and mountains. Lake Lanier, Lake Allatoona and Lake Oconee create steady demand for boat storage, and the North Georgia mountains attract motorcycle riders and drivers who want to keep a car closer to the good roads.
  • Track access. With Road Atlanta in Braselton and Atlanta Motorsports Park in Dawsonville, Georgia has two major road courses within an easy drive of the northern suburbs.
  • Community. Car condo communities increasingly function as social clubs, with owner events and cars and coffee meetups. For many buyers, that is as valuable as the square footage.

Georgia-Specific Buying Considerations

Heat, humidity and climate control

Georgia's challenge is the opposite of the Midwest's. Long, hot, humid summers are hard on paint, leather, rubber and electronics, and moisture can encourage corrosion and mildew. Ask whether units are insulated, whether HVAC or mini-split systems are allowed or included, and whether the building has a vapor barrier and good drainage. If you plan to store a high-value collection, climate control is close to essential.

Storms and insurance

Severe thunderstorms, hail and occasional tornadoes are part of life in Georgia. Make sure your contents and vehicle policies cover the cars and property inside the unit, and ask the association what its master policy covers (usually the building shell and common areas, not your contents or interior improvements).

New construction risk

Because so much of Georgia's inventory is new, you will often be buying from a developer, sometimes before the building is finished. Garage Lofts at Mall of Georgia is under construction, and several selling projects are still adding phases. Ask how deposits are held, what the delivery timeline is, what specifications are guaranteed in writing, and when control of the HOA passes from the developer to owners. Our buying guide covers every document to request.

Use rules, rentals and business use

Declarations vary widely. Some Georgia projects welcome small business use; others are strictly for personal storage and hobby use. Overnight occupancy is generally prohibited. If you hope to rent your unit when you are not using it, confirm rental rules first; Stables Motor Condos in Alpharetta, where both buying and renting are offered, is one example of a community where rentals are part of the model. See our renting guide for more.

Property taxes and dues

A garage condo is taxable real property in Georgia, and county assessments and millage rates differ across Fulton, Cobb, Cherokee, Gwinnett, Jackson, Dawson and the other counties on this list. Ask for the most recent tax bill on a comparable unit and the association's current budget before you sign.

Choosing Between Georgia's Communities

A simple way to narrow the field:

  • Daily access near home: choose the closest North Metro project to where you live, whether that is Alpharetta, Acworth, Buford or Ball Ground. A unit you can reach in 15 minutes gets used; one an hour away often does not.
  • Track focus: Atlanta Motorsports Park Trackside Garage Condos in Dawsonville or Stables Motor Condos in Braselton near Road Atlanta.
  • Boats and lake life: Buford near Lake Lanier, Acworth near Lake Allatoona, or Park Court on Lake Oconee.
  • Mountain base: The Toy Barn of Dillard.
  • Lowest entry price: Storage Caves Buford or HomeBase Flex in Ringgold.
  • Top-tier space: Alpharetta Personal Warehouse or the larger units at Storage Caves Buford and Garage Lofts at Acworth Summit.

Once you have a shortlist, visit in person, measure the door and ceiling, and talk with current owners about how the association runs. Already own a unit in Georgia? You can list your unit on AutomotiveCondos.com to reach buyers and renters searching for garage condos in the Atlanta area.

Questions to Ask Every Georgia Developer

  • How many units are in the project in total, and how many remain?
  • What are the exact door, ceiling and depth dimensions for the unit I am considering?
  • Is HVAC included, optional or allowed, and what is the electrical service per unit?
  • What are the current monthly HOA dues, and what do they cover?
  • When will control of the association transfer to owners?
  • Are rentals and business use permitted under the declaration?
