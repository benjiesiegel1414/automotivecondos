Garage Condos for Sale Near Me: How to Actually Find One in 2026

November 6, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

Type "garage condos for sale near me" into Google and you'll get a mess: a few expired LoopNet listings, a Zillow page full of regular condos that happen to have garages, and maybe one developer site for a community three states away. The frustrating truth is that garage condos are one of the fastest-growing niches in real estate — and one of the hardest to shop for.

Why garage condos are so hard to find

Three reasons this market hides from normal search tools:

  • They're often commercial property. Many garage condo communities are zoned and platted as commercial condominiums. That pushes listings to LoopNet and Crexi — platforms most car collectors never think to search — instead of the residential MLS that feeds Zillow and Realtor.com.
  • Developers sell direct. New communities typically sell through their own websites and sales offices, with no MLS listing at all. If you don't know a community exists, you'll never see its inventory.
  • Resales happen inside the walls. In sold-out communities, units frequently change hands owner-to-owner — someone mentions at cars-and-coffee that they're selling, and it's gone before it was ever listed.

The right way to search

Step 1: Map every community in your market. Before you shop units, you need the full list of communities within driving distance. That's exactly what our state-by-state directory and national map are for — every community we track, including resale-only communities that never advertise and planned projects you can get into at pre-construction pricing.

Step 2: Sort by status. "Selling" communities have developer inventory today. "Resale only" communities are sold out, but units trade regularly — these are often the best-located, most established buildings. "Under construction" and "Planned" are your early-pricing opportunities.

Step 3: Get on lists. For sold-out communities you love, contact the association or developer and ask to be notified of resales. Serious buyers who've expressed interest hear about units days before the public does.

Step 4: Check the commercial platforms too. Once you know a community's exact name, search it on LoopNet and Crexi — resale units that do get formally listed usually land there.

What "near me" really means in this market

Garage condos cluster around wealth, car culture, and friendly zoning — which means coverage is uneven. Florida alone has 100+ communities; some entire states have one or two. If your metro has nothing, look at the nearest hub: collectors routinely buy units 45–90 minutes from home, because the unit is a destination, not a daily commute.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't garage condos show up on Zillow?

Many garage condos are classified as commercial condominiums, so they get listed on commercial platforms (LoopNet, Crexi) or sold directly by developers rather than through the residential MLS feeds that power Zillow and Realtor.com. Resales often trade owner-to-owner inside the community before they're ever publicly listed.

How do I find garage condos in my state?

Start with a state-level directory. AutomotiveCondos.com tracks 270+ communities across 42 states, including communities that are selling, resale-only, under construction, and planned — so you can see every option in your market, not just the ones paying to advertise.

Can I buy a garage condo before it's built?

Yes. Most new communities sell units in pre-construction phases, and early-phase pricing is usually the lowest the community will ever offer. The trade-off is that delivery dates can slip by months or more.
