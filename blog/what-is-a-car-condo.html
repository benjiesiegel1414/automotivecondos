What Is a Car Condo? Deeded Garage Ownership, Explained

November 6, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

A car condo (also called a garage condo, auto condo, or motor condo) is a garage unit you own outright — deeded real property, exactly like a residential condominium. You take title, you can finance it with a mortgage, improve it however you like, lease it out, and sell it when you're done. That single fact separates a car condo from every storage unit you've ever rented.

How ownership works

A developer builds a community of garage units — typically 20 to 300 of them — and sells each unit individually. You get a deed, you pay property taxes, and a condo association maintains the shell: roof, exterior walls, common drives, insurance on the structure, and usually gated security. Association dues are typically modest compared to a residential HOA because there's not much to maintain.

Inside your walls, it's yours. Owners finish units with epoxy floors, lifts, mezzanine lofts with lounges, bathrooms, A/C, and in some communities a small office for a business.

Who buys them

  • Collectors who have outgrown the home garage and want their cars in one secure, climate-controlled place.
  • RV and boat owners — a 14- or 16-foot door clears most motorhomes, and HOA-restricted neighborhoods can't complain about what's parked inside.
  • Small businesses and tradespeople, where zoning allows — detailers, shops, contractors who need deeded space instead of a lease.
  • Investors who lease units to all of the above, or buy pre-construction in early phases.

Car condo vs. storage unit

A rented storage bay is an expense that goes up every year and builds nothing. A car condo is an asset: you control your costs, you build equity, and well-located communities have appreciated meaningfully as the category has grown. Many sold-out communities now trade actively on the resale market.

The community factor

Don't underestimate this one: most car condo communities turn into car clubs. Cars-and-coffee mornings, member events, neighbors who will talk camshafts with you for an hour. For a lot of owners, that's half the purchase.
