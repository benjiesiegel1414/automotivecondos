The Best States for Garage Condos in 2026, Ranked by the Data

November 6, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

We track 270+ garage condo communities across 42 states — selling, resale-only, under construction, and planned. That dataset gives a pretty clear picture of where this market actually lives. Here's the 2026 landscape.

Tier 1: Florida — the capital

Around 100 communities, from $180K resale bays in Punta Gorda to $2M+ trackside units at Tampa's Motor Enclave. Southwest Florida (Naples–Fort Myers–Punta Gorda) is the deepest sub-market in America; Tampa Bay carries the highest medians; and the pipeline is enormous — including a planned 400-unit motorsport resort in Naples. Deep resale liquidity, real price history, and real competition among developers.

Tier 2: Texas and Arizona — the growth engines

Texas (30+ communities) is the platform-builder state: Garages of America alone operates 14 locations across DFW, Houston, and Austin, with COTA trackside condos topping $1.25M. Arizona (20+) is brand country too — Toy Barn's nine Phoenix-area locations, LuxeLocker's buy-or-rent model, and new luxury projects in Scottsdale. Both states add projects quarterly.

Tier 3: Established regional hubs

  • Minnesota / Wisconsin: where the category was born — AutoMotorPlex Chanhassen remains the spiritual home, with new phases in Medina and projects in Lakeville and Big Lake.
  • Illinois: Chicago's suburbs are busy — Iron Gate Naperville, plus new builds in Barrington, Lake Zurich, and Mundelein.
  • Michigan: M1 Concourse's 250+ units beside a private track, with Motorsports Gateway Howell under construction.
  • Colorado / Idaho / Washington: steady mountain-west growth from Denver's metro clusters to Boise, Spokane, and a 100-unit motor-and-yacht project on Seattle's Salmon Bay.
  • The Carolinas & Georgia: racing country delivers — Charlotte's speedway corridor, Storage Caves' multi-state expansion, Atlanta Motorsports Park trackside units.

The sleepers

Two surprises from the data: Alaska — Anchorage alone has five communities (long winters, big toys); and New Hampshire's lake country, with three communities serving boat-season storage demand. Meanwhile, genuinely underbuilt territory remains: Las Vegas proper, Southern California's inland metros, Nashville, and most of the Northeast outside New Jersey — markets with the car culture and the money, but hardly any product. Developers, take note.

How to use this

Pick your state on the map, compare communities by status and price, and remember the pattern that holds everywhere: the best-located communities sell out, go resale-only, and appreciate. The buyers who do best get into good locations early. Start here: all 42 states.

Frequently asked questions

Which state has the most garage condos?

Florida, by a wide margin — our directory tracks roughly 100 communities there, more than triple any other state, concentrated in Southwest Florida, Tampa Bay, and the southeast coast.

What states are garage condos growing fastest in?

Texas and Arizona stand out: Texas has 30+ communities led by the 14-location Garages of America platform, and Arizona has 20+ including multi-location brands like Toy Barn and LuxeLocker, with several more under construction in both states.

Are there garage condos outside the Sun Belt?

Yes — Minnesota and Wisconsin pioneered the concept (AutoMotorPlex), Chicago's suburbs have an active cluster, and communities now exist everywhere from New Hampshire lake country to Anchorage, Alaska.
