Garage Condos in Washington State: 2026 Market Guide

November 7, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

Washington is one of the deepest garage condo markets in the country, and one of the most spread out. The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 26 garage condo communities across the state, with 1,257 known units among the communities that publish unit counts. That is a larger known unit base than Colorado or California in our directory. The market runs from the Canadian border at Blaine and Bellingham, through the Puget Sound suburbs, over the Cascades to Cle Elum and Lake Chelan, across the Columbia Basin to Quincy and the Tri-Cities, and east to Spokane and Pullman. If you are searching "garage condos Washington State," "garage condos Spokane," or "garage condos Tri-Cities," this guide covers every market.

Pricing spans a wide range: resale units in Pullman trade around $92,000, new units at Crescent Bar near Quincy start around $107,000, and a new motor and yacht club in Seattle starts in the mid-$600,000s. Every community named below is listed in the full Washington directory, and you can see all 26 on the interactive map.

Why Washington Has So Many Garage Condos

Washington's market was not built by car collectors alone. It was built by boats, RVs, and the gear that comes with living near water and mountains.

Boat and water culture. Puget Sound, Lake Washington, Lake Chelan, and the Columbia and Snake river systems give Washington one of the strongest recreational boating cultures in the country. Boats need dry, secure off-season storage, and many households simply do not have the space. That demand shows up directly in the directory: Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club in Seattle markets itself to both car and boat owners, and the four Quincy-area communities sit near the Columbia River at Crescent Bar.

RV and camping culture. Washington's national parks, national forests, and high desert draw a large RV and travel trailer crowd. City and HOA rules often prohibit parking an RV in the driveway, so owners look for deeded storage. RV Home Storage Condominiums in Sultan sells deeded RV garages, and several eastern Washington projects are built with tall doors for motorhomes and fifth wheels.

Rain, and lots of it, on the west side. Western Washington's long wet season is hard on cars, motorcycles, and anything with wood or upholstery. A dry, enclosed bay with power for a battery tender or dehumidifier protects a vehicle in ways a carport or tarp cannot. East of the Cascades, the concern shifts to winter snow and summer heat, but the result is the same: secure indoor space is worth paying for.

The GarageTown footprint. Washington is a home base for the GarageTown brand, one of the best-known names in Pacific Northwest garage condos. The directory lists five GarageTown communities in the state: Federal Way (established), Kennewick and Spokane Valley (resale only), and Liberty Lake and Pasco (planned). That concentration of a single recognizable product type has helped make garage condo ownership a familiar idea to Washington buyers, which supports a healthy resale market.

The Washington Market at a Glance

The 26 Washington communities break down by status this way: 12 are actively selling, 8 are resale only, 3 are planned, 2 are under construction, and 1 is established. With nearly half the market actively selling new units, Washington buyers have more new-construction choice than in many other states.

Western Washington

Whatcom County: Bellingham, Ferndale, and Blaine

The far northwest corner of the state is one of Washington's most active garage condo pockets, with five communities and about 286 known units. Buyers here include boat owners on Bellingham Bay and Birch Bay, collectors, and small business owners who use flex units as shop space.

If you are shopping garage condos in Bellingham, two communities are actively selling:

  • Irongate RediSpace Condominium: 51 units, priced from about $246,000 to $340,000.
  • Carolina Business Park Condominium: 44 units, pricing on inquiry.

Just north, the market for garage condos in Ferndale includes the largest Whatcom County community:

  • Pioneer Flexspace: 108 units, resale only, around $279,000 to $290,000.
  • Flexspace North: 40 units, selling new at about $328,000 to $335,000.

The roughly $40,000 to $50,000 gap between Pioneer resales and Flexspace North new units is a useful benchmark for how much Washington buyers pay for new construction over a comparable resale.

Near the border, My Garage at Birch Bay in Blaine has 43 units trading as resales around $160,000 to $170,000, the lowest published pricing on the west side of the state.

Puget Sound: Seattle, Federal Way, Sultan, and Gold Bar

The Seattle metro has the most expensive land in Washington, and its garage condo inventory reflects that. There are four communities here:

  • Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club in Seattle: 100 units, under construction, priced from the mid-$600,000s. This is the state's top-tier product and the only one in the directory positioned as a combined car and yacht club.
  • GarageTown Federal Way: 67 units, an established community where units trade as resales. It is the closest GarageTown to Seattle and Tacoma.
  • RV Home Storage Condominiums in Sultan: 35 deeded RV garages, resale only.
  • Gold Bar Garage Condominiums: 23 units, resale only, inquire for listings.

Sultan and Gold Bar sit along US-2 at the western edge of the Cascades, which makes them practical for Snohomish County owners who want RV or toy storage on the way to Stevens Pass and eastern Washington.

Central Washington

Cle Elum and Lake Chelan

Cle Elum sits on I-90 just east of Snoqualmie Pass, a natural staging point between Seattle and the dry side of the state. It has become a second-home and recreation hub, and its garage condo market reflects that. For garage condos in Cle Elum, two communities are selling:

  • Monster Garage Condos: 116 units, priced around $415,000 to $420,000. One of the largest active projects in the state.
  • Cle Elum Garage Condos: priced from about $209,000.

Farther north, Chelan Luxury Garages has 58 units under construction and selling pre-construction. Lake Chelan is one of the state's premier boating and vacation destinations, and lakeside second-home owners have limited options for boat and vehicle storage close to the water.

Quincy and Crescent Bar

Quincy is the surprise of the Washington directory. This small Columbia Basin city has four communities and 201 known units, driven by Crescent Bar (a Columbia River resort area), vineyard country, and the concert crowds at the nearby Gorge Amphitheatre in George. It also has some of the lowest new-construction pricing in the state. For garage condos in Quincy:

  • Crescent Bar Garage Condominiums: 64 units, selling from about $107,000.
  • Trinidad Garages: 39 units, selling at about $130,000 to $132,000.
  • Garages at Crescent Ridge Ranch: 51 units, resales around $160,000 to $237,000.
  • Garages at The Vineyard: 47 units, selling around $349,000.

For a boat owner who spends summer weekends on the Columbia, a Quincy unit offers storage right where the boat gets used, at a fraction of west side pricing.

Eastern Washington

The Tri-Cities: Richland, Kennewick, and Pasco

Richland, Kennewick, and Pasco sit where the Columbia, Snake, and Yakima rivers meet, and the area has a large boating, fishing, and RV community. It also has a strong GarageTown presence. For garage condos in Richland:

  • Fat Cat Garages Tri-Cities: 38 units, selling at about $356,000 to $365,000.
  • LuxeLocker Richland: selling, and one of the few Washington communities that offers both purchase and rental options.

Across the river, GarageTown Kennewick has 71 units trading as resales, and GarageTown Pasco is planned and listed as coming soon. For buyers who want to try before they buy, LuxeLocker's rent-or-own model is worth a look.

Spokane, Spokane Valley, Liberty Lake, and Chattaroy

Greater Spokane is tied with Whatcom County as the largest region in the Washington directory, with five communities. If you are shopping garage condos in Spokane:

  • Fat Cat of Spokane: 29 units, selling around $200,000.
  • Garage Lodge Storage Condos: selling from about $250,000.
  • GarageTown Spokane Valley: 91 units, resales around $314,000.
  • GarageTown Liberty Lake: planned, coming soon.
  • Sunset Storage Condos Chattaroy: 122 units planned, the largest known unit count of any Washington community in the directory, with pre-construction pricing.

Spokane draws from both sides of the Idaho line, and with lakes like Coeur d'Alene and Priest Lake close by, boat and toy storage is a big part of demand. Snowy winters also make indoor storage close to home valuable for anyone who keeps a summer car, a motorcycle, or a side-by-side.

Pullman

GarageMahal Pullman has 20 units trading as resales around $92,000, the lowest published price in the Washington directory. Pullman is home to Washington State University, and a unit here is a low-cost way to own deeded storage in the Palouse.

Washington pricing splits cleanly into three tiers:

  • Value tier (under $175,000): Pullman, the Quincy-area projects at Crescent Bar and Trinidad, and Birch Bay resales. Mostly smaller or more basic units, often in recreation markets.
  • Core tier ($200,000 to $365,000): Most of the state, including Bellingham, Ferndale, Spokane, the Tri-Cities, and the entry level at Cle Elum. Expect larger bays, better finishes, and active associations.
  • Premium tier ($400,000 and up): Monster Garage Condos in Cle Elum and Terminal One in Seattle. Larger units, resort or urban locations, and club-style amenities.

West side versus east side is the biggest price driver. A buyer who can store a boat or RV in Quincy or Spokane instead of the Seattle metro may save several hundred thousand dollars. Our cost guide explains how size, ceiling height, and finish affect price per square foot.

What to Check Before Buying a Washington Garage Condo

Door height for boats and RVs

Because so much Washington demand comes from boats and RVs, door height is the first spec to verify. A boat on a trailer with a tower or a bimini, or a Class A motorhome, needs a much taller opening than a car. Measure the full height on the trailer, including any tower or antenna, and confirm the clear opening of the overhead door, not just the ceiling height. Also confirm the unit depth if you are storing a long boat or trailer.

Moisture control on the west side

West of the Cascades, ask about insulation, ventilation, and whether owners may run dehumidifiers. Condensation on a cold slab can be hard on cars and upholstered boats. Confirm each unit has its own electrical service or at least dedicated outlets for battery tenders and climate equipment.

Snow and winter access on the east side

In Spokane, Cle Elum, Chelan, and the Columbia Basin, winter snow is a factor. Ask who plows the drive aisles, how quickly, and whether the cost is included in dues. Heated units or permission to install a heater are worth a premium for anyone storing a car or motorcycle they plan to use through the winter.

Use rules and flex units

Many Washington communities, especially in Whatcom County, are sold as flex or business park condominiums. That can be a plus if you want a workshop or a small business base, but it also means the rules may differ from a pure car storage community. Read the declaration and rules for restrictions on overnight stays, commercial activity, and modifications. Our buying guide lists the documents to request and the questions to ask.

Rental demand

Boat and RV storage is in steady demand across the state, particularly in recreation markets like Quincy, Chelan, and the Tri-Cities. If you plan to rent a unit out, confirm leasing is allowed and look at what competing indoor storage charges nearby. Our renting guide covers pricing, leases, and insurance.

How to Choose a Washington Market

  • Lowest cost of entry: GarageMahal Pullman resales and new units at Crescent Bar in Quincy.
  • Boat storage near the water: Quincy (Columbia River), Chelan (Lake Chelan), Whatcom County (Bellingham Bay and Birch Bay), and Terminal One in Seattle.
  • RV storage near Puget Sound: RV Home Storage Condominiums in Sultan and Gold Bar Garage Condominiums along US-2.
  • Established brand with resale history: GarageTown in Federal Way, Kennewick, and Spokane Valley.
  • Largest new projects: Sunset Storage Condos Chattaroy (122 units planned), Monster Garage Condos in Cle Elum (116 units), and Terminal One (100 units).
  • Luxury and club amenities: Terminal One Motor + Yacht Club in Seattle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many garage condo communities are there in Washington State?
