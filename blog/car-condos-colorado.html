Car Condos in Colorado: 2026 Market Guide

November 7, 2026 · AutomotiveCondos.com

Colorado is one of the most active car condo markets in the Mountain West. The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 23 garage condo communities across the state, with 736 known units among the communities that publish unit counts. The market is concentrated along the Front Range, where the Denver metro alone holds the majority of projects, but it reaches north to Fort Collins and Loveland, south to Colorado Springs, and over the Continental Divide to Glenwood Springs and Grand Junction. If you are searching "car condos Colorado" or "garage condos Denver," you are looking at a market with an unusually wide price range: resale bays in Englewood start around $55,000, while new luxury units in Glenwood Springs start from $449,000.

This guide walks through every Colorado market in the directory, explains what drives pricing in each, and shows where new inventory is coming from in 2026 and 2027. Every community named here is listed in the full Colorado directory, and you can see them plotted on the interactive map.

Why Colorado Buyers Want Garage Condos

Colorado's demand comes from three overlapping groups, and each one has a specific problem a deeded private garage solves.

Collectors and enthusiasts protecting cars from weather. The Front Range sits in one of the most hail-prone corridors in the country, and a single summer storm can total a classic's paint and glass. Winter adds snow, freeze-thaw cycles, and the chemical de-icers used on Colorado roads, which are hard on undercarriages and brake lines. A collector car that never leaves a covered, insulated bay from November to April simply ages more slowly. For owners in townhomes or HOA neighborhoods with one or two garage spaces, the only real alternative is a rented storage unit with no power, no ownership, and no equity.

The outdoor toy crowd. Colorado's lifestyle runs on gear: off-road rigs, side-by-sides, snowmobiles, campers, rafts, mountain bikes, and ski equipment. Many metro HOAs prohibit parking trailers or RVs in driveways, which pushes owners toward secure off-site storage. A garage condo with a tall door can hold a camper van or a trailer-mounted snowmobile setup in winter and a boat or side-by-side in summer. Several Colorado communities, including Indiana Street RV Plaza Condos in Golden, were designed explicitly with RV-height bays.

Small business owners and hobbyists. Colorado garage condos are often marketed as "storage condominiums" or "warehouse condos," and many buyers use them as contractor shops, inventory space, or hobby workshops (subject to each association's rules). Communities like Canyon View Warehouse Condos in Golden and Personal Warehouses Fort Collins straddle the line between car storage and light commercial flex space, which broadens the buyer pool at resale.

Layer onto that a decade of Front Range population growth, with steady in-migration along the I-25 corridor from Fort Collins to Colorado Springs, and you get a market where industrial land is expensive, zoning is tight, and newly built private garage space sells at a premium.

The Colorado Market at a Glance

The directory's 23 Colorado communities break down by status as follows: 9 are actively selling new units, 7 trade on the resale market only, 2 are established communities with occasional listings, 2 are under construction, and 3 are planned. That mix matters. Resale-only communities give you a finished product you can tour today, often at a lower price per square foot, while selling and pre-construction projects give you new construction, modern amenities, and a choice of unit sizes.

Denver Metro: Where Most of the Market Lives

Golden and the West Metro

Golden is the busiest single city in the Colorado directory, with four communities. Three are established resale products and one is new construction, which makes Golden a good place to compare old and new side by side. If you are shopping car condos in Golden, start here:

  • Canyon View Warehouse Condos: 40 units, resales listed at roughly $300,000 to $325,000. Warehouse-style units suited to collectors who also want a workshop.
  • Indiana Street RV Plaza Condos: 27 units, resales around $240,000 to $250,000. Built with RV and trailer storage in mind.
  • 44th Storage Condominiums: 40 units, resale only. Inquire for current listings.
  • Golden Garages by Playground: 53 units, under construction and selling pre-construction. This is the largest new-build pipeline in the west metro.

Nearby, Garage Point Colorado in Morrison is one of the largest active projects in the state at 89 units, priced from about $220,000 to $435,000. That range is wide because Garage Point offers multiple unit sizes, so it works for a single-car buyer and a multi-car collector alike. Morrison sits at the foothills on the way to the mountains, a practical staging point for anyone storing ski, off-road, or camping gear. In Lakewood, Garage Condos by My Dream Storage is selling; pricing is available on inquiry. And in Arvada, Playground Garages Arvada is in the planned stage.

Englewood, Centennial, and the South Metro

The south metro around the Dove Valley area and Centennial Airport is home to Colorado's most mature resale market, and it holds the cheapest entry point in the state. If you are researching car condos in Englewood, these three resale-only communities define the market:

  • Dove Valley Storage: 176 units, the largest community in the Colorado directory by known unit count. Resales range from about $55,000 to $83,000, which makes it the lowest-cost way to own deeded garage space anywhere in the state.
  • Windmill Creek Storage Condos: 62 units, resales around $172,000 to $180,000.
  • ManCaves at Dove Valley: 64 units, resales around $295,000. As the name suggests, these units skew toward finished, lounge-style builds.

The spread inside one neighborhood, from roughly $55,000 at Dove Valley Storage to roughly $295,000 at ManCaves, shows how much unit size and finish drive value. A small storage bay and a large finished man cave a few blocks apart are almost different asset classes.

Next door, the market for car condos in Centennial includes The Garage Den, a resale-only community, and Playground Garages Centennial, which is actively selling. Pricing for both is available on inquiry, so contact the listing teams through the community pages for current numbers.

Broomfield and the North Metro

Broomfield sits between Denver and Boulder along US-36, and it has one established community and one in the pipeline. For car condos in Broomfield, Depot Hill Garage Condos is the benchmark: 33 units with an average price around $390,000, one of the higher averages in the metro. Playground Garages Broomfield is planned and will add new inventory to a submarket that currently has very little.

Just northeast of downtown, The Garage Society in Commerce City is selling with pricing from about $242,000, a mid-market option for buyers who want new construction without the premium of a west metro address.

Colorado Springs

Colorado Springs has three communities in the directory and the strongest new-build value on the Front Range. If you are shopping car condos in Colorado Springs, the headline is MyGarage Northcrest: 71 units, actively selling, priced from about $175,000 to $258,000. That is roughly $50,000 to $70,000 below comparable new product in the Denver metro, and it is new construction rather than resale.

My Car Barn is a small, three-unit boutique project that is selling (inquire for pricing), and Playground Garages Colorado Springs is planned and taking pre-construction interest. The Springs has a large military and veteran population, a big off-road and camping culture thanks to Pikes Peak and the surrounding national forest, and plenty of HOA neighborhoods with tight driveway rules, so demand for secure toy storage runs deep.

Northern Colorado: Fort Collins and Loveland

Northern Colorado is a smaller market in the directory but a growing one. Personal Warehouses Fort Collins is selling eight units, and Motorplex at Centerra in Loveland is an established community near the I-25 and US-34 interchange. Both list pricing on inquiry. With limited supply and a fast-growing population between Fort Collins, Loveland, and Greeley, buyers here should expect units to move quickly when they do come up.

The Western Slope: Glenwood Springs and Grand Junction

West of the Continental Divide, the market looks different. Buyers here are often second-home owners, outdoor enthusiasts, and collectors who want storage near their mountain or desert property.

In Glenwood Springs, Citadel Luxury Garages is selling 20 units from $449,000, the highest published starting price in the Colorado directory. Glenwood is a resort town close to Aspen and Vail traffic, and luxury garage product here prices like the mountain real estate around it.

Grand Junction offers the opposite end: Western Slope pricing with new construction. If you are researching car condos in Grand Junction, Grand Garages is selling 50 units at about $275 to $295 per square foot, which works out to roughly $206,000 for a 20 by 35 foot unit. Playground Garages Grand Junction is under construction. Grand Junction is a hub for off-road, mountain biking, and river sports, so expect plenty of trailers and side-by-sides alongside the cars.

Three tiers emerge from the data:

  • Entry tier (under $100,000): Small storage bays, almost entirely resale, led by Dove Valley Storage. Good for a single collector car or a motorcycle collection, with little room for a lounge or lift.
  • Core tier ($170,000 to $300,000): The bulk of the Colorado market, including new construction at MyGarage Northcrest, Grand Garages, Garage Point, and The Garage Society, plus Golden resales. Expect enough space for two to four cars, power, and often room for a mezzanine.
  • Premium tier ($390,000 and up): Larger or more finished units at Depot Hill, the upper end of Garage Point, and Citadel in Glenwood Springs. These units typically support lifts, finished lounges, and serious collections.

The Playground Garages Pipeline

One developer name appears six times in the Colorado directory: Playground. Its projects span Golden (under construction, 53 units), Centennial (selling), Grand Junction (under construction), Colorado Springs (planned), Broomfield (planned), and Arvada (planned). That is more than a quarter of the state's communities under one brand, and it represents most of the new supply coming online over the next few years.

For buyers, a multi-location developer has practical upsides. Floor plans, finish packages, and association documents tend to be consistent from site to site, which makes comparison shopping easier. A recognizable brand can also help at resale, because buyers in one city already know the product from another. The tradeoff is timing: planned projects can shift schedules, so read the purchase agreement closely for deposit terms, completion deadlines, and what happens if construction is delayed. Our buying guide covers the questions to ask before you sign a pre-construction contract.

What to Check Before Buying a Colorado Garage Condo

Heat, insulation, and snow load

Colorado winters make heating and insulation more important than in the Sun Belt markets. Ask whether units are insulated, whether gas or electric heat is allowed, and whether the association permits individual heaters. Batteries, fluids, and tires all fare better above freezing. Also ask about snow removal: who plows the drive aisles, how quickly, and whether the cost is covered by dues. A unit you cannot reach after a storm is not much use in January.

Door size and ceiling height

If you plan to store a camper van, a lifted truck, a trailer, or an RV, door height is the single most important spec. Standard car condo doors work for sedans and sports cars, but RV-oriented product like Indiana Street RV Plaza Condos exists for a reason. If you want a two-post or four-post lift, you need ceiling clearance to match. Measure your tallest vehicle and confirm the clear door opening, not just the ceiling height.

Permitted uses

Many Colorado projects are zoned light industrial and sold as warehouse or storage condominiums. That gives you flexibility for a workshop or small business, but it usually means overnight occupancy is prohibited and certain uses (painting, welding, auto repair for hire) may be restricted. Read the declaration and rules before you plan a buildout. Our buying guide lists the documents to request.

HOA dues and reserves

Dues typically cover exterior maintenance, insurance on common elements, security systems, landscaping, and snow removal. Ask for the current budget and reserve study, and look at how dues have trended. Older resale communities can carry special assessments for roofs or paving; newer ones may have artificially low dues during the developer period.

Rental potential

If you are buying partly as an investment, check whether the association allows leasing and on what terms. In a market where HOA neighborhoods restrict trailer and RV parking, rentable secure storage tends to find tenants. Our renting guide walks through pricing, leases, and insurance for owners who want to rent out a unit.

How to Choose a Colorado Market

  • Lowest cost of entry: Englewood resales, starting with Dove Valley Storage.
  • Best new-build value: MyGarage Northcrest in Colorado Springs and Grand Garages in Grand Junction.
  • Mountain access from Denver: Golden and Morrison, on the west side of the metro toward I-70.
  • Largest active project: Garage Point Colorado in Morrison, with 89 units and a wide size range.
  • Luxury and resort: Citadel Luxury Garages in Glenwood Springs and Depot Hill in Broomfield.
  • Future supply: Playground's planned and under-construction sites in Golden, Grand Junction, Colorado Springs, Broomfield, and Arvada.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many car condo communities are there in Colorado?

The AutomotiveCondos.com directory tracks 23 car and garage condo communities in Colorado as of November 2026, with 736 known units among communities that publish unit counts. Seventeen are in the Denver metro, three are in Colorado Springs, two are in Grand Junction, and the rest are in Fort Collins, Loveland, and Glenwood Springs.

How much does a garage condo cost in Denver?

Denver metro pricing ranges widely. Resale storage bays at Dove Valley Storage in Englewood list around $55,000 to $83,000, while most core product (Windmill Creek, Indiana Street RV Plaza, The Garage Society, Garage Point, Canyon View) falls between about $172,000 and $325,000. Larger or more finished units, such as those at Depot Hill in Broomfield (about $390,000 average) and the upper end of Garage Point (up to about $435,000), sit in the premium tier.

What is the cheapest car condo in Colorado?

Dove Valley Storage in Englewood, with resales from roughly $55,000 to $83,000, is the lowest published entry point in the Colorado directory. For new construction, MyGarage Northcrest in Colorado Springs starts around $175,000, and Grand Garages in Grand Junction works out to roughly $206,000 for a 20 by 35 foot unit.

Are there new garage condos being built in Colorado?

Yes. Golden Garages by Playground (53 units) and Playground Garages Grand Junction are under construction, and Playground has planned projects in Colorado Springs, Broomfield, and Arvada. Several other communities, including Garage Point Colorado in Morrison, MyGarage Northcrest in Colorado Springs, and Grand Garages in Grand Junction, are actively selling new units.

Can I store an RV or camper in a Colorado garage condo?

Some Colorado communities are built for it. Indiana Street RV Plaza Condos in Golden was designed with RV and trailer storage in mind, and several larger projects offer tall-door units. Always confirm the clear door opening and ceiling height against your vehicle's height, and check the association rules on vehicle types and length before you buy.
