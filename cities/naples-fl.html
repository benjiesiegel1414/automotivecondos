The Naples car condo market

Naples is home to 15 of the 103 car condo communities we track in Florida — about 15% of the state's market. 11 are actively selling, under construction, or taking reservations, so new-build units are available, while 4 trade on the resale market. None of the Naples communities we track advertise rentals, though individual owners sometimes lease privately. Tracked pricing in Naples runs roughly $539K – $930K, depending on unit size, finish, and community amenities.
