New to car condos?

A car condo is a garage you own.

Think of it like a condo for your cars: a private, deeded garage unit inside a secure community. Owners use them to store collections, build out a lounge, run a workshop, or rent them out for income.

🔑

Deeded, like real estate

You own it outright, can finance it, and can sell it later.

🏎️

Built for the collection

Tall doors, room for a lift, and often a mezzanine loft.

🛡️

Secure, managed community

Gated access, cameras and an HOA that handles the grounds.

💲

Typically $250K at the median

Across the 234 communities that publish pricing.
