Seller FAQ

Who can list a unit?

Owners, landlords and their agents. If you own (or are authorized to sell or rent) a garage condo, car condo, storage condo or motor condo unit anywhere in the US, you can list it.

What does it cost?

$29 per month, billed by Stripe. That includes your own listing page, placement on your community and state pages, and your phone, email and website shown right on the listing. There's no commission and no contract.

How fast does my listing go live?

Within one business day of checkout. We review every submission, build your page, and email you the link.

Do you take a commission when it sells?

No. Buyers and renters contact you directly, and the deal is between you and them. We never take a cut of your sale or lease.

Can I list a unit for rent instead of for sale?

Yes. Choose For rent on the form and enter your monthly rate. Renters searching your area and our car condos for rent page will see it.

My community isn't in your directory. Can I still list?

Yes. Type the community name with the city and state, and we'll add the community to the directory along with your listing.

Can I change my price or photos later?

Anytime. Email contact@automotivecondos.com or reply to your listing email with the changes and we'll update your page.

How do I cancel?

Email contact@automotivecondos.com or reply to your Stripe receipt and we'll cancel your subscription and take the listing down. Sold already? Tell us and we'll mark it sold.

Why does my card statement say Dividend Empire?

Dividend Empire is the parent company behind AutomotiveCondos.com, so payments are processed under that name (TOPDIVIDENDTOOLS.COM on some statements).

I'm a developer with a whole community to sell. What should I use?

Take a look at our Featured Community spots and Launch Package. They put your entire project on the homepage, at the top of your state page and in front of our buyer email list.
