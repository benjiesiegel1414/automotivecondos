About this community

Toy Cave puts 56 large garages just inside the Houston core, off W. 12th Street near the Galleria and Memorial. Every unit comes finished with its own HVAC, and the 3,400 sq ft clubhouse has an F1 racing simulator, bar and private rooms.

Toy Cave Luxury Garages is a RV, boat & toy storage community in Houston, Texas. The community spans 56 individually owned units. Toy Cave Luxury Garages is under construction. Pre-construction buyers typically get the best pricing and unit selection before delivery.
