About this community

The Thermal Club is the country's best-known motorsports residential community: about 426 acres in the Coachella Valley with multiple private tracks, where owning a property is how you get track access. Villas come with garages large enough for serious collections.

The Thermal Club is a car condo / motor condo community in Thermal, California. The Thermal Club is an established, operating community. Units change hands by resale and occasionally by lease — inquire to hear what's available.
