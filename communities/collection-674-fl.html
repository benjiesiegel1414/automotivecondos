About this community

Collection 674 in Oviedo is Central Florida's first purpose-built luxury car condo community: 34 gated, deeded units on Aloma Avenue east of SR 417. The specs are strong for the price, with 21 ft clear ceilings, 14 ft doors and a clubhouse with racing simulators.
