About this community

Sebring Motor Garages is the official motor condominium partner of Sebring International Raceway, home of the 12 Hours of Sebring. The gated, 15-acre community is less than a mile from the track, and owners can get up to 25 track days a year through the Sebring Driving Club.

Sebring Motor Garages is a car condo / motor condo community in Sebring, Florida. The community spans 157 individually owned units. Sebring Motor Garages is actively selling units — developer inventory is available now, which usually means first pick of sizes and locations within the community.
