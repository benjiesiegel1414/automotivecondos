About this community

Circuit Florida in Auburndale, between Tampa and Orlando, is a true live-at-the-track community. Its 75 townhome-style garage condos are zoned for full-time living and line a 1.7-mile, 14-turn road course with more than 40 feet of elevation change, which is rare in Florida.

Circuit Florida Car Condos is a motorsports / trackside community in Auburndale, Florida. The community spans 75 individually owned units. Circuit Florida Car Condos is actively selling units — developer inventory is available now, which usually means first pick of sizes and locations within the community.
