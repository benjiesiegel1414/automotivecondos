About this community

Toy Barn's ninth Valley location is planned for Mack Innovation Park at Loop 101 and Bell Road. It is the company's flagship: 64 suites in phase one, about 135 at build-out, and Toy Barn's first members-only rooftop lounge, with views of the McDowell and Camelback Mountains. Toy Barn has built 543 units across 700,000 sq ft since 2010, and its first seven locations sold out.

Toy Barn North Scottsdale is a RV, boat & toy storage community in Scottsdale, Arizona. The community spans 64 individually owned units. Toy Barn North Scottsdale is in the planning / pre-development stage. Early reservations often lock pre-construction pricing before the first shovel hits dirt.
