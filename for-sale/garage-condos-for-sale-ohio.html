Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Ohio?

We track 26 car condo and garage condo communities in Ohio. 17 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 9 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Ohio?

Published pricing across 16 Ohio communities runs $66K to $550K. The lowest tracked starting price is at Catawba Storage Condominiums (I–VI) in Port Clinton (Resales ~$66K–$194K; phase VI under construction). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Ohio garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Port Clinton (4), Medina (2), Lakeside Marblehead (2), Columbia Station (2). The full Ohio directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
