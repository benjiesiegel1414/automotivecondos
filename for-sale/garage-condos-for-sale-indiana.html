Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Indiana?

We track 7 car condo and garage condo communities in Indiana. 5 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 2 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Indiana?

Published pricing across 6 Indiana communities runs $40K to $1.3M. The lowest tracked starting price is at Big Boyz Toys Jimmerson Lake in Angola (~$39.9K–$100K+ depending on upgrades). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Indiana garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Westfield (2), Indianapolis (1), Angola (1), Fort Wayne (1). The full Indiana directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
