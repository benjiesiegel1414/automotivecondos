Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in California?

We track 21 car condo and garage condo communities in California. 9 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 12 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in California?

Published pricing across 8 California communities runs $300K to $3.5M. The lowest tracked starting price is at The Caves in Murrieta (From the $300s). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most California garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Monterey (2), Palm Desert (2), Beaumont (1), Calabasas (1). The full California directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
