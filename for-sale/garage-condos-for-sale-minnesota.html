Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Minnesota?

We track 43 car condo and garage condo communities in Minnesota. 32 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 11 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Minnesota?

Published pricing across 16 Minnesota communities runs $45K to $1.7M. The lowest tracked starting price is at Stor Smart North in Carlos (Alexandria area) (2026 sales $45K–$318K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Minnesota garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Independence (2), Lakeville (2), Prior Lake (2), Delano (2). The full Minnesota directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
