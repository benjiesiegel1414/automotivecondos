Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Washington?

We track 26 car condo and garage condo communities in Washington. 17 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 9 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Washington?

Published pricing across 16 Washington communities runs $92K to $600K. The lowest tracked starting price is at GarageMahal Pullman in Pullman (Resales ~$92K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Washington garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Quincy (4), Bellingham (2), Cle Elum (2), Ferndale (2). The full Washington directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
