Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Michigan?

We track 34 car condo and garage condo communities in Michigan. 25 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 9 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Michigan?

Published pricing across 11 Michigan communities runs $95K to $1.1M. The lowest tracked starting price is at Zeeland South Storage Condominium in Zeeland (~$95K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Michigan garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Grand Haven (3), Zeeland (2), Holland (2), Traverse City (2). The full Michigan directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
