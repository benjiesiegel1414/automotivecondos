Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Florida?

We track 103 car condo and garage condo communities in Florida. 73 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 30 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Florida?

Published pricing across 43 Florida communities runs $175K to $1.8M. The lowest tracked starting price is at Toy Vault Condo in Fort Myers ($175K–$500K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Florida garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Naples (15), Fort Myers (9), Sarasota (7), Jacksonville (6). The full Florida directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
