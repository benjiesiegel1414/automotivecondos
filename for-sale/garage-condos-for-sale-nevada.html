Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Nevada?

We track 8 car condo and garage condo communities in Nevada. 6 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 2 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Nevada?

Published pricing across 1 Nevada communities runs $900K. The lowest tracked starting price is at The Stack Auto Lofts in Henderson (From the high $900Ks). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Nevada garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Henderson (3), Las Vegas (1), Mesquite (1), Pahrump (1). The full Nevada directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
