Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Colorado?

We track 57 car condo and garage condo communities in Colorado. 30 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 27 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Colorado?

Published pricing across 13 Colorado communities runs $55K to $528K. The lowest tracked starting price is at Dove Valley Storage in Englewood ($55K–$83K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Colorado garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Broomfield (4), Golden (4), Centennial (3), Colorado Springs (3). The full Colorado directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
