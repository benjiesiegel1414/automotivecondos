Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Arizona?

We track 71 car condo and garage condo communities in Arizona. 35 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 36 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Arizona?

Published pricing across 29 Arizona communities runs $30K to $899K. The lowest tracked starting price is at Premier Storage Condominiums of Yuma (I & II) in Yuma (~$30K–$94K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Arizona garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Lake Havasu City (19), Scottsdale (13), Phoenix (6), Chandler (5). The full Arizona directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
