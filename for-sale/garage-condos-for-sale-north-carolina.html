Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in North Carolina?

We track 11 car condo and garage condo communities in North Carolina. 9 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 2 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in North Carolina?

Published pricing across 1 North Carolina communities runs $360K. The lowest tracked starting price is at The Garages at LKN in Mooresville (From $360K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most North Carolina garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Concord (3), Mooresville (3), Bolivia (1), Charlotte (1). The full North Carolina directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
