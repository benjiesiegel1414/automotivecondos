Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in Texas?

We track 52 car condo and garage condo communities in Texas. 34 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 18 are established communities where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in Texas?

Published pricing across 13 Texas communities runs $185K to $1.3M. The lowest tracked starting price is at Big Tex Garage Condos in Tomball ($184,500–$189,500 per unit (Phase 2)). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most Texas garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Cypress (4), San Antonio (4), Dallas (3), Houston (3). The full Texas directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
