Frequently asked questions

How many garage condos are for sale in South Carolina?

We track 9 car condo and garage condo communities in South Carolina. 8 are selling new units, under construction, or taking reservations, and 1 is an established community where units change hands on the resale market.

How much does a garage condo cost in South Carolina?

Published pricing across 6 South Carolina communities runs $285K to $550K. The lowest tracked starting price is at Motor-Vault Lakeside at Lake Keowee in Six Mile (From $285K). Most other communities price on inquiry, so unlock each listing for current numbers.

Where are most South Carolina garage condos located?

The biggest clusters are in Mount Pleasant (2), Awendaw (1), Bluffton (1), Charleston (1). The full South Carolina directory lists every city we track.

Should I buy new construction or resale?

New construction lets you pick a unit and finish early, often at pre-construction pricing, but you wait for the build. Resale units are available now and you can see the finished community first, but inventory is thin, so it pays to inquire with several communities at once.
