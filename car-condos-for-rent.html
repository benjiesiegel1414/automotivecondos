Frequently asked questions

Can you rent a car condo?

Yes. 34 of the 558 communities we track offer units for rent as well as for sale, across 17 states. Renting is a good way to try garage condo life before buying.

How much does it cost to rent a car condo?

Rents vary widely by market, unit size and finish. Storage style units in smaller markets rent for far less than finished luxury units in major metros. Open any community below to unlock its direct listing and current rates.

What if there is no rental community near me?

Individual owners in for-sale communities often lease their units privately. Check the full state directory and inquire with the communities closest to you.
