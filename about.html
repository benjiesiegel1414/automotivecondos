About AutomotiveCondos.com

Car condos are one of the fastest-growing corners of real estate: deeded garage units that collectors, RV owners, and small businesses actually own — with lifts, lofts, and a community of people who care about what's parked inside.

The problem: the market is scattered. Every community markets itself, listings hide in local MLS feeds and Facebook groups, and there's no one place to see what exists, what's selling, and what's coming.

That's what we're building. AutomotiveCondos.com tracks every car condo community in the country — selling, resale-only, under construction, and planned — organized by state and city, with the details that matter: unit counts, status, and real-world pricing.

Buying? Start with your state. Selling? List your unit where buyers are already comparing communities.

Questions, corrections, or a community we're missing? Email us at contact@automotivecondos.com
